A make.fun entry for **Dad Joke Showdown**: one original dad joke, at most 200 characters, written to be told out loud.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I told my wife she should embrace her mistakes. She hugged me.

The joke is 62 characters: a clean turn (embrace = literally hug; the husband is the mistake), tight, punchline at the end, and built for a father's delivery across the dinner table.
